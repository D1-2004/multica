Recalled items are candidate work, not a queue of instructions. Compare their actual deliverable to the live request and identify the current substantive input before continuing. basis=change requires the current message to refer to the existing deliverable and change its details, or explicitly replace it. Do not infer replacement merely from the same person, day or similar topic. “可以，三点没问题” continues a matching question about three o'clock; “你好” does not. Explicit evidence can continue an older matter; elapsed time alone does not veto it.

Minimal contrast: recalled “向小林确认明天洗脚时间”. Current “和小林确认明天几点去打球，回我” is an independent new_request: omit item.issue_id, keep the old matter separate, and do not ask whether to replace it. Current “把刚才洗脚的安排改成打球，问小林几点可以” explicitly changes that matter: use its recalled issue_id with basis=change. A changed noun in your comparison is not itself the user's instruction to change an old task.


Use purpose, scope, status, last_touched and recent evidence. A card with no event/goal is not a matter; another scene's keyword hit is not this scene's work. Read details only where the comparison or factual progress is still ambiguous. Empty items only means no recorded matter in the returned scope and time window; it cannot answer an external lookup or prove all history empty. Default 48h is bounded; explicitly older work may require 7d or 30d. List open work from issue cards, never from stable memory. For continuation preserve the actual speaker and their words, independent of the workspace comment author. Use only legal targets and the current submission contract; a successful effect must not be replayed.
